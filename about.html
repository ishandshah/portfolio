About

Ishan Shah

Senior Software Engineer · Distributed systems, event-driven platforms, and reliability

Engineering for the moment things go wrong.

I am a Senior Software Engineer working on distributed systems for billing, inventory, fulfillment, and security analytics. Across PayPal, Nordstrom, and Securonix, my work has centered on a practical question: how do we keep business state correct when services fail, events arrive twice, or a platform needs to recover?

At PayPal, I contribute to cloud-native usage and subscription billing. At Nordstrom, I worked on fulfillment optimization, allocation, inventory, and financial data pipelines. At Securonix, I built ingestion and search capabilities for security investigation.

My work combines hands-on Java and streaming-system development with architecture reviews, operational readiness, and mentoring. I write and speak about recovery contracts, evidence-driven incident response, and the boundaries that keep AI-assisted engineering accountable.
